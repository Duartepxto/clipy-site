APK · Android 8 or newer

Clipy connected to your computer
  1. 1

    Download and install

    Open Clipy.apk. The first time, Android asks whether your browser may install apps: tap Settings → Allow from this source and go back.

  2. 2

    Show the QR on your computer

    In Clipy on your computer: Settings → Phone → Pair a phone, with Android app selected. The code works once and lasts 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Scan it with the app

    Open Clipy on your phone and tap Scan the QR. It stays paired with that computer and remembers it next time.

Connect from another network

Use the same Wi-Fi at home. Away from home, connect through Tailscale or a configured Cloudflare One private route before pairing.

No account

The QR gives the phone a key of its own. Unpair it anytime in Settings → Phone on your computer.

iPhone or another computer?

Pick Browser / iPhone on your main computer. Scan the QR with an iPhone or open the pairing address on your second computer. Keep Clipy running on the main computer.
